References

Archives and resources used to research this discography

Broadcast Archive

BBC Genome Archive
Searchable digitisation of BBC Radio Times listings from 1923 to 2009; useful for confirming the broadcast dates of BBC performances.
Inathèque
Catalogue of France's national audiovisual archive (INA), covering historic French radio and television broadcasts.
Rai Teche
Search portal for the historic radio and television archive of Rai, Italy's state broadcaster.
Beeld & Geluid
Catalogue of the Netherlands Institute for Sound and Vision, the Dutch national broadcast archive.
Svensk Mediedatabas
Swedish National Library's database of radio, television and film holdings, including broadcast recordings.
Swiss National Sound Archives
Switzerland's national sound archive, cataloguing historic recordings including radio broadcasts.

Paper Archive

Newspapers.com
Subscription database of digitised historic newspapers, useful for locating concert reviews and listings.
The Times Archive
Searchable digital archive of The Times (UK) dating back to 1785, useful for concert reviews and listings.
Papers Past
Free archive of historic New Zealand newspapers maintained by the National Library of New Zealand.
Radiocorriere
Archive of Rai's weekly programme guide (Italy's equivalent of Radio Times), useful for confirming Italian broadcast dates.

Festival Archive

Prague Spring
Archive of past programmes from the Prague Spring International Music Festival.
Wiener Festwochen Archiv
Archive of the Vienna Festival (Wiener Festwochen) programme history.
Lucerne Festival
Works archive listing repertoire performed at the Lucerne Festival.
Aldeburgh Festival
Digitised programme books from the Aldeburgh Festival, hosted by Britten Pears Arts.
Salzburg Festival
Official performance archive of the Salzburg Festival.

Orchestra Archive

NY Phil archives
New York Philharmonic's Leon Levy Digital Archives, with searchable performance history back to 1842.
Archief Concertgebouworkest
Royal Concertgebouw Orchestra's searchable concert archive.
Boston Symphony Orchestra
Boston Symphony Orchestra's digital performance history archive.
Orchestre de la Suisse Romande
Orchestre de la Suisse Romande's concert archive.
Chicago Symphony Orchestra
Chicago Symphony Orchestra's Rosenthal Archives performance history database.
Gewandhaus Orchestra
Leipzig Gewandhaus Orchestra's concert archive.
The Hallé
Performance archive of the Hallé Orchestra, Manchester.
Vienna Philharmonic Orchestra
Vienna Philharmonic's concert archive.

Hall Archive

Carnegie Hall
Carnegie Hall's Performance History Search database, covering concerts held there since 1891.
100 лет Санкт-Петербургской филармонии
Centenary archive site for the St Petersburg Philharmonic, with historical concert records.
Wiener Konzerthaus
Official site of the Vienna Konzerthaus, including its concert history.
Tokyo Bunka Kaikan
Performance database for Tokyo Bunka Kaikan, a major concert hall in Tokyo.

Other Resources

CHARM
Online discography and library of digitised 78rpm recordings from the AHRC Research Centre for the History and Analysis of Recorded Music, hosted by Royal Holloway.
ArchiveGrid
OCLC's aggregated index of archival collection descriptions from libraries, museums and archives worldwide; useful for locating performers' personal papers.
Operabase
Database tracking opera and concert performances worldwide, useful for cross-referencing live performance dates.
Classite
Large collaborative online database of classical music recordings and performer discographies, with a collectors' marketplace.
